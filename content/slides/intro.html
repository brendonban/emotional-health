The one-session version covers all three GLF principles at an introductory level: presence, the line of choice, and emotional health levels, then closes with one practical step. The four parts run 20, 25, 25 and 20 minutes, 90 in total. The pilot opens with this session. It works as a standalone exposure session for large audiences and as a recruitment route into the four-session programme. Adjust timings to the setting.
