Care and safeguarding

Screening and referral

Intake screening, with referral to clinical care when a group isn't the right fit.

Clinical oversight

Groups facilitated by counsellors working under clinical supervision.

Crisis pathways

Malaysian helplines: Talian Kasih (15999), Befrienders (03-7627 2929), and 999 for emergencies.

Privacy and consent

Data handled under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010, with consent.
