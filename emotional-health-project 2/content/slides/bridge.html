The first three rows follow GLF's three core principles in the order the booklet presents them: presence and whole-body thinking (balancing head, heart and body), personal responsibility (the line of choice between automatic blame, defend, deny and justify responses and a constructive one, made by activating the inner observer), and emotional health levels (moving up through vertical, internal development as coping strategies and defence mechanisms are let go). The last row applies them to leadership, which GLF says runs from parent to president. GLF's concepts give everyday language to what young adults struggle with, and the Above the Line workshops turn that language into practice.
