GLF tools: Above & Below the Line card sort (tests.globalleadershipfoundation.com/cards/a-btla/above-below-line.php), sorting behaviour cards into Like Me and Not Like Me; Emotional Health Level card sort (tests.globalleadershipfoundation.com/cards/ehl-4l/ehl-4l.php), rating how often each behaviour shows up; Global Emotional Intelligence Test (tests.globalleadershipfoundation.com/geit/eitest.html), 40 forced-choice items on Goleman's four EI quadrants. The GLF tools speak directly to the programme's content; DASS-21 adds a psychometrically validated outcome. Reach measures: the number of young adults reached through outreach, the number of workshops delivered (both the 1.5-hour session and the four-session programme), and an average session rating of 4 out of 5 as a simple quality check. How the pilot-year targets add up (October 2026 to September 2027): 6 introductory 1.5-hour sessions (2 in the pilot, 4 in Q2) at about 25 people each reach 150 young adults. If 30% of them join the full programme, that fills 4 four-session groups of about 12 (1 in the pilot, 3 in Q2). 6 intro sessions plus 4 programmes makes 10 workshops. The 6 introductory sessions run across 3 university campuses (1 in the pilot, 2 more in Q2), with remaining sessions at workplace or community events. Adjust if group sizes or venues change. Measures are taken at the first and last session, with consent.
