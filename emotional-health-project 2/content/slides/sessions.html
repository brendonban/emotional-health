Click to climb the staircase one session at a time, echoing GLF's emotional health ladder. Sessions 1 to 3 follow GLF's three core principles in the booklet's order: presence and whole-body thinking, personal responsibility and the line of choice, and emotional health levels. Stigma and loss of face sit within sessions 2 and 3 as examples of below-the-line responses and defences. Session 4 applies the principles to leadership, which GLF says runs from parent to president.
